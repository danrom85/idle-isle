# Idle Isle Vision

> A tiny world that lives while you work.

Idle Isle is a peaceful living-world desktop companion. It is not a conventional game and should never pressure the viewer to perform, optimize, collect, or win.

## Product promise

A glance at Idle Isle should reveal a small, coherent world continuing its life without waiting for the user.

## Guiding principle

> Every feature must make the island feel more alive—not merely more complicated.

## Design values

- **Alive over busy.** Motion should communicate life, intention, weather, or time—not visual noise.
- **Meaningful over random.** Events may be probabilistic, but should feel plausible within the world.
- **Peaceful over exciting.** Surprise is welcome; urgency is not.
- **Timeless over trendy.** The experience should remain charming after years of use.
- **Original over nostalgic.** Classic desktop companions are inspiration, never a template to copy.
- **Handcrafted over generated.** Runtime stories, animations, sounds, and visual assets are authored deliberately.

## Interaction model

The primary experience is observation. There are no scores, quests, inventories, streaks, or required clicks. Optional settings may change presentation and accessibility, but the island does not depend on user input to live.

## The five-second test

For every proposed feature, ask:

> Would this make someone smile if they looked at the screen for five seconds?

If the answer is unclear, the feature needs refinement or does not belong.

## Emotional target

Idle Isle should feel like an aquarium, terrarium, campfire, or bonsai: a calm presence that rewards occasional attention without demanding it.
